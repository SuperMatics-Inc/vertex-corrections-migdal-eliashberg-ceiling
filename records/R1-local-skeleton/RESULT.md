# G1-VERTEXBOUND — day-1 PARTIAL: centered local ceiling and bare-block obstruction

**PROVED (B + C).** Take a symmetric flat DOS on \([-W,W]\) (so \(A=1\)), even-frequency singlet pairing, fixed nonnegative phonon spectrum, and the self-consistent first-crossed skeleton with three independently DOS-integrated internal lines. On every centered (\(\chi_n=0\)), particle-hole-symmetry-preserving normal branch with positive reflected widths, set \(m=\Omega_{\max}/W\), \(M=\int\Omega^2\,d\lambda(\Omega)\), and \(\rho=m\sqrt\lambda\). Then

\[
 k_BT_{\rm lin}\le C_\infty\sqrt{AM}\,[1+30((1+\lambda)\rho+\rho^2)].
\]

This is valid for Einstein or general bounded-support spectra, with an explicit small correction at fixed \(\lambda\) as \(m\to0\). It is not uniform-small in joint limits where \((1+\lambda)\rho\not\to0\); the upper bound itself still holds. Centered normal solutions with positive reflected widths exist at each energy \(\tau\in[C_\infty\sqrt M,2C_\infty\sqrt M]\) when \((1+\lambda)\rho\le0.01\). No statement is made about other branches or real-axis causality.
At each fixed finite \(\lambda\), the uninflated ceiling itself holds for sufficiently small \(m\), by absorbing the form error into the strictly positive scalar Einstein gap at the ceiling.

**Vertex-specific contribution.** The three anomalous insertions and the crossed normal mass combine into a signed square of a four-corner Matsubara difference, with exact cancellation when either phonon transfer is zero. This yields a uniform-in-temperature form estimate on the same centered crossed normal widths, without a separate ME fixed-point comparison or an isolated-eigenvalue assumption. A separate bare finite-temperature witness obstructs a termwise absolute Schur bound. See [RIGOR.md](RIGOR.md).

**PROVED obstruction (A).** At fixed \(\lambda\) and \(\tau=k_BT=\Omega\), the dynamic forward triangle on a flat box band tends to \(\lambda\) (bare lines) or \(\lambda/(1+\lambda)\) (ME-dressed lines) as \(m=\Omega/\epsilon_F\to0\); the bare simple-cubic triangle also tends to \(\lambda\) with bounded DOS. These disprove the respective DOS-only pointwise \(O(\lambda m)\) estimates. An absolute Cooper-weighted end-insertion block has norm bounded below independently of \(m\) for the **bare flat-band example only**. No such block claim is made for the dressed or cubic examples, and no estimate restricted to a fully crossed self-consistent lattice branch or after summing signed terms is ruled out.
