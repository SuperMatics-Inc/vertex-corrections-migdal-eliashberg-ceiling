## Numbered defects
1. **Restrict the theorem to centered normal branches** (`RIGOR.md:18,27–31`, `RESULT.md:3`; P2). Positive reflected Matsubara widths do not imply a zero real self-energy: ordinary reflection allows an even real shift \(\chi_n\). The integral of \((\xi+\chi_n)/(u_n^2+(\xi+\chi_n)^2)\) over the flat band vanishes only at \(\chi_n=0\). Eq. (3), the pairing weights, and the existence proof all use that centered ansatz. State \(\chi_n=0\) explicitly (the particle-hole-symmetry-preserving branch); do not infer absence of other branches or real-axis causality from the positive box.
2. **Count coincident corners with multiplicity in Eq. (5)** (`RIGOR.md:42–45`; P2). For \(j=1,l=-1\), \(b=n\). Take \(y_n=2,y_{n-1}=y_{n+1}=-1\); the squared alternating difference is 36, while four times the sum over the *set* of vertices is 24. The correct bound is four times \(|y_n|^2+|y_a|^2+|y_b|^2+|y_c|^2\), including repeated occurrences. The separate reanchoring of each occurrence is valid and restores the same 16, hence the same 30.
3. **Scope the absolute-Schur KILL to the verified propagators** (`RESULT.md:14–16`, `RIGOR.md:104–116`; P2). The dressed flat example is a pointwise ME-branch result; the Cooper-block norm lower bound is bare and the genuinely dispersive cubic example is pointwise only. Say so in the KILL sentence and replace “This is not an artifact” with “The pointwise obstruction also holds in 3D.” No estimate restricted to a fully crossed self-consistent lattice branch, or taken after summing signed terms, is ruled out by these fixtures.
4. **Separate the theorem from earlier local models and forward limits** (`RESULT.md:20`; P2). Cite [Paci et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.65.012512) for the half-filled constant-DOS local vertex and numerical \(T_c\); cite [1995 I](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.52.10516), [II](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.52.10530) and [Chubukov et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.01281) for established forward/typical-momentum distinctions. Grimaldi et al. give a nonvanishing dynamic limit, not just the triangle definition. The narrower contribution is the matched-branch signed form, explicit all-temperature bound, and finite-temperature absolute-block witness; no priority claim is warranted.

## Analytic audit
- Eq. (1) is a well-defined local electronic skeleton with fixed phonons: per-spin DOS integrates to 1, \(V=K/N_0\) has energy units, and \(m=\Omega_{\max}/W\) uses the half-width (equal to bare edge-to-Fermi energy here, not away from half filling). Both signs and transfer conventions match Schrodi Eq. (4) after independent local DOS integration, not their momentum-conserving isotropic FSR approximation. It is a truncation, not a bound on omitted diagrams or actual transition temperatures.
- Conjugating the two end Nambu factors gives normal coefficient \(-i\) and anomalous coefficient \(y_a-y_b+y_c\) before the common negative cubed prefactor. Hence the crossed normal term enters with the same negative coefficient as the anomalous combination in (3). Multiplying the normal fixed-point equation by \(y_n\) and subtracting pairing produces the alternating four-corner row. The four bijective reanchorings yield \(-\eta/4\) times the signed square (also for complex test vectors). Either static transfer vanishes before taking absolute values. With (2 above) corrected, \(\sum_{j,l\ne0}K_jK_l=4L^2\), four reanchored corner bounds give \(16\eta L^2\sum b_n|y_n|^2\le4X\|x\|^2\); folding halves form and norm equally.
- The static part of the crossed normal row is \(-\eta\lambda b_n(\lambda b_n^2+2\sum_{j\ne0}K_jb_{n-j}^2)\); the nonstatic row costs at most \(X=4\eta L^2\). Thus \(s_n\le w_n+\lambda+2L+X\) and \(1-b_n\le2\tau s_n/W\). The off-diagonal row mass is \(2\sum_{j\le n}K_j+K_{2n+1}\le w_nK_1\). Group weighted rows by transfer: up plus down (or minus reflected) gives \(2w_n\) at each positive transfer; omitted Hankel diagonal restores \(w_nK_{2n+1}\). Endpoint differences vanish by \(jK_j\to0\). This proves (6) with the stated \(D_*\); comparison uses absolute values on tests, not a Loewner claim about entrywise order.
- \(S(1)\) has diagonal \((K_{2n+1}-2\sum_{j\le n}K_j)/w_n\), exactly the [Rens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20496) scalar operator (Thms. 3–6), not the finite-band ME normal operator. Its soft comparator has top \(C_\infty^2 M/\tau^2\). Coarse constant bounds also follow on paper: the soft trial \(x_n=\sqrt{w_n}(1,1/4,1/10)_n\) gives \(g>129/100\); a weighted row bound with \(w^{-1/2}\) gives \(g<25/16\) (row 0: first four off-diagonals plus tail \(1/32\); row 1: retain negative diagonal; later rows: \(13/36+17/48+43/60\)). Thus \(9/50<C_\infty=\sqrt g/(2\pi)<1/5\) without rounding the decimal.
- Basel and the moment ceiling give \(L\le(4/3)r^2\), \(\tau L/W\le\rho r/4\), and \(X\le(20/3)\rho r^3\). Since \(K_1\le L\), \(D_*\le(\rho r/2)(3+\lambda+2L+X)\); adding \(4X\) yields \(r[(\lambda/2+59/2)\rho+(10/3)\rho^2]\le r\epsilon\). At \(r(1+\epsilon)\le1\), \(r^2+\epsilon r<1\). The uniform row bound on \(s_n-w_n\) and strictly positive widths make the change back to the raw gap norm boundedly invertible. Jensen/Einstein comparison and temperature monotonicity bound \(S(1)\) by \(1-\delta_\lambda\) for all \(\tau\ge\tau_0\); \(\epsilon<\delta_\lambda\) proves the uninflated ceiling at fixed \(\lambda\), not uniform as \(\lambda\to\infty\).
- Box: \(\eta\le2\rho\), \(\eta(\lambda+4L)\le(32/3)(1+\lambda)\rho<1\), and \(X\le1/15\). After combining the static terms, the negative remainder is at most \(K_1+X<4/5+1/15<7/8\). The continuous finite normal maps preserve the positive box; diagonal extraction and \(\ell^1\) transfer envelopes give a centered solution at each claimed temperature, not a continuous, unique or globally existing branch.
- **Not a new spectral principle.** At fixed \(\lambda\), strict ME margin plus an already established uniform vanishing form perturbation would imply stability. The vertex work is obtaining that perturbation on the actual normal widths, with exact cancellation of \(K_0^2\) and \(K_0L\) and the stated joint scaling. A naive absolute bound \(\eta(\lambda+2L)^2\) has worse coupling dependence and grows with temperature; generic perturbation theory does not supply the claimed guard.

## Obstruction and literature
- Flat \(q=0\): the opposite-sign \(p=-1\) term is \(\lambda b_0/s_0\); same-sign divided differences have magnitude \(\le2N_0/W\), so their sum costs \(\le3\lambda m\). At fixed \(\tau/\Omega=1\), the ME row telescopes to \(s_0\to1+\lambda\). For the bare block the resolvent Lipschitz loss is at most \(3\lambda/16\) on the patch, leaving \(\ge\lambda/2\). The Cooper integral exactly offsets patch shrinkage and gives \(\lambda K_1\arctan(1/16)/(2\pi)\). Simple-cubic bounded continuous DOS follows from the three \(L^{3/2}\) arcsine factors; the Poisson limit and the summable \(K\) envelope establish its bare pointwise limit. Transfer continuity makes the obstruction one of essential supremum, not a measure-zero exception.
- [Schrodi et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.102.024503), Eqs. (4), (20), Sec. III.C, already use full electronic self-consistency and analyze momentum limits, but retain joint momentum constraints. [Miller et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.58.14498), Sec. [Freericks](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.50.403) and [Hague](https://doi.org/10.1088/0953-8984/17/37/005) provide earlier local/cluster calculations.
